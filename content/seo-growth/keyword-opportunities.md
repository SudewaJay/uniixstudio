# Keyword opportunities: publishing cycle, Oct–Nov 2026

Prepared 18 October 2026.

## How the opportunities were scored

**No keyword volume or difficulty data was available.** Search Console isn't authenticated, and no keyword tool was connected. Every score below is a qualitative judgement from five inputs, and the evidence for each is stated:

1. **Business fit (1–5):** how directly the topic leads to a Uniix Studio service.
2. **Commercial intent (1–5):** how likely a searcher is to be choosing a supplier or making a buying decision.
3. **SERP gap (1–5):** how weak, dated or off-target the current results looked in our web searches. US-based search tools were used, so Sri Lankan results may differ.
4. **Evidence advantage (1–5):** whether Uniix has verified, first-hand project evidence that competitors can't copy.
5. **Cannibalisation risk (Low / Med / High):** overlap with the 63 existing posts and the location pages.

Opportunity score = fit + intent + gap + evidence (out of 20), lowered for Medium or High cannibalisation risk.

## Evidence for the selected five

### 1. Online booking system Sri Lanka
- **SERP observation:** results for "online booking system website Sri Lanka" were dominated by a 2016 news story, airline FAQs, university theses and an unmaintained hotel plugin. None of them helps a business owner decide.
- **Evidence advantage:** CricBook (full two-sided booking platform), RentMyCar.lk (a documented decision *not* to build a booking engine), Wasana Drivers (WhatsApp quotation flow).
- **Angle:** "build, buy or wait", which is honest about when not to build.

### 2. Medical lab / clinic website design Sri Lanka
- **SERP observation:** a freelancer portfolio piece, hospital press releases from several years ago and a non-Sri Lankan agency page. No practical guide written for Sri Lankan labs or clinics.
- **Evidence advantage:** St Luke's Medical Laboratory, the agency's most detailed and measurable case study.
- **Timeliness:** the PDPA provisions take effect on 1 January 2027 (ft.lk, July 2026 gazette).

### 3. Payment gateway Sri Lanka
- **SERP observation:** the top comparison results were a 2019 readme.lk article, a 2023 list and vendor content (crypto gateways, low-quality "Infolanka" pages). Fees in most results were outdated.
- **Verified facts used:** Stripe's global page (Sri Lanka not listed), Shopify Payments' supported countries (Sri Lanka not listed), Shopify's third-party fee page, PayHere's published fees, and WEBXPAY's and PAYable's own feature lists, all checked 18 Oct 2026.
- **Cannibalisation control:** the e-commerce guide's payment section is broader; the new article is the dedicated answer and is linked from it (see the internal linking plan).

### 4. Multilingual website Sri Lanka
- **SERP observation:** vendor SEO pages repeating unsourced statistics, with no technical guidance on hreflang, Unicode or typography for Sinhala and Tamil.
- **Verified facts used:** Google's hreflang and multi-regional documentation, Google's spam policies (automated translation), and the LK Domain Registry's IDN information.
- **Angle:** the legacy non-Unicode font problem, and designing for the longest language. These are practical Sri Lankan details competitors don't cover.

### 5. .lk domain registration
- **SERP observation:** results dominated by domainoffer.net price-aggregator pages for overseas registrars. No clear explanation of the registry's own categories, steps or ownership risks.
- **Verified facts used:** domains.lk homepage prices, registration procedure and naming structure.
- **Role:** top-of-funnel. It catches people at the very start of building a website and passes them to web design packages and the contact page.
