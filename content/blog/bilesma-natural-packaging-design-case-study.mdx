**Bilesma Natural (Pvt) Ltd** is a Sri Lankan natural beauty company built on a simple promise — help people *let their inner beauty shine*. The products lived up to it. The packaging didn't. Uniix Studio redesigned Bilesma Natural's product packaging from a plain kraft carry bag into a premium botanical brand experience that finally looks the way the products feel. This is how we did it — and why **product packaging design** is one of the highest-leverage investments a product business in Sri Lanka can make.

> **Studio:** Uniix Studio (Design pillar)

## The challenge: great products, forgettable packaging

Bilesma Natural came to us with a recognisable problem for growing Sri Lankan product brands. The product quality was there. The packaging was holding the brand back.

The original carry bag was a standard brown kraft (SOS-style) paper bag with a single printed band wrapped around the middle and a small logo inside an outlined badge. On its own, it was a perfectly functional bag. As a *brand asset*, it had three problems:

- **It said nothing about "natural beauty."** The utilitarian blue band and bare kraft read like a grocery or takeaway bag — not a wellness or skincare product. The most important word in the brand, *natural*, was nowhere to be seen visually.
- **It was invisible on shelf.** Next to competitors, there was nothing to make a customer pause, pick it up, or remember it. No tagline, no colour story, no point of difference.
- **It wasted a free marketing surface.** A carry bag travels home with the customer and sits on a counter or in a bag for days. The old design carried no website, no contact details, and no reason to reorder.

In short: the packaging was a cost, not an asset.

## Our approach: six decisions that made the difference

Uniix Studio's design process is structured, not decorative. Every choice below was made to solve a specific brand problem.

### 1. We repositioned the format from "functional" to "premium"

Moving from bare kraft to a clean white bag immediately repositioned Bilesma Natural from "everyday" to "considered." White creates space, signals cleanliness and care, and gives the botanical artwork room to breathe — exactly the cues a natural skincare buyer is looking for.

### 2. We told the "natural" story visually

Instead of *saying* natural, we *showed* it. A cascade of watercolour botanicals — monstera and palm fronds — runs down one side of the bag, wrapping the brand in the tropical, fresh, plant-derived feeling at the heart of the product. This is the single biggest shift: the packaging now communicates the brand promise before a single word is read.

### 3. We locked the palette to brand-aligned greens

The old blue band fought the brand. We replaced it with a botanical green system that ties every touchpoint to nature, wellness, and skincare. Colour consistency is what turns a logo into a *brand* people start to recognise.

### 4. We gave the brand an emotional promise

We placed the line **"Let Your Inner Beauty Shine"** front and centre, emphasising *BEAUTY* in brand green. Packaging that carries a promise sells; packaging that only carries product just ships. This one line gives the customer a feeling to associate with the brand.

### 5. We turned the bag into a marketing and trust asset

A clean green footer band now carries the full business identity — `www.bilesmanatural.lk`, `customercare@bilesmanatural.lk`, both phone lines, and the Nugegoda address. Every bag that leaves the store becomes a reorder prompt and a credibility signal that travels into the customer's home.

### 6. We built a two-panel brand system

The bag works from any angle on shelf. One face leads with the emotional message; the other is a clean logo brand panel with delicate line-art botanicals and soft amber and sage accent leaves — a more sophisticated, layered look that reinforces the identity without shouting.

## Why packaging design matters for Sri Lankan product brands

Packaging is often the *first* physical interaction a customer has with your brand — and frequently the *last* thing they see before deciding to buy again. For Sri Lankan retail, cosmetics, and food brands competing on crowded shelves and in marketplaces, well-considered packaging design does three jobs at once:

1. **It differentiates.** A distinctive look is the cheapest, most durable competitive advantage you can buy.
2. **It signals quality.** Customers infer product quality from packaging quality — fairly or not.
3. **It markets for free.** Good packaging travels, gets photographed, gets reordered, and keeps your contact details in the customer's hands.

A small investment in packaging design routinely returns more than the same spend on ads, because the asset works on every single unit you sell — for as long as you sell it.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does product packaging design cost in Sri Lanka?

Product packaging design in Sri Lanka typically ranges from around LKR 40,000 for a single-item label or bag design to LKR 250,000+ for a full packaging system across multiple product lines, depending on scope, the number of formats, and whether brand identity work is included.

### What makes good packaging design for a natural or beauty brand?

Good natural-beauty packaging communicates the brand promise visually — through palette, illustration, and material — before any text is read. It should look premium, stay consistent across every product, and double as a marketing surface by carrying the brand's contact and web details.

### How long does a packaging redesign take?

A focused single-format packaging redesign usually takes two to four weeks, including discovery, concept, revisions, and print-ready files. Larger systems across multiple product lines take longer.

### Can you redesign packaging without changing our whole brand?

Yes. A packaging redesign can refresh the look and shelf presence while keeping your existing logo and core identity, or it can be paired with a fuller brand identity update if the current branding is holding you back — as it was in the Bilesma Natural project.
