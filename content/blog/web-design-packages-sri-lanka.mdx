"Send me your web design packages" is one of the first things a Sri Lankan business owner asks — and the quotes that come back range from LKR 40,000 to LKR 800,000 for what sounds, on the surface, like the same thing. It isn't. The gap isn't markup; it's what's included, and crucially, what's quietly left out.

This guide breaks down exactly what you should get at every price point, so you can compare quotes honestly and match the package to what your business actually needs — without overpaying or, worse, underbuying something you'll have to rebuild in a year.

## Why "packages" are so confusing

Web design packages are hard to compare because two quotes at very different prices can both say "professional website" — while including completely different things. The low quote might exclude content writing, SEO, testing, and support. The high quote might include all of them plus custom design and integrations.

The headline price tells you almost nothing. What matters is the inclusions. So this guide is organised around what you should actually get at each tier — use it as a checklist against any quote you receive.

## The tiers, and what each should include

### Tier 1: Budget — LKR 40,000–80,000

**What it typically is:** A template-based website, lightly customised with your branding and content.

**What's usually included:**
- A pre-made template adapted to your brand
- A handful of pages (home, about, services, contact)
- Mobile responsive
- Basic contact form
- You supply all content and images

**What's usually NOT included (watch for these):**
- Custom design (it's a template)
- Content writing (you write everything)
- Real SEO setup
- Extensive testing
- Meaningful post-launch support
- Integrations beyond a basic form

**Right for:** A small business that needs a basic professional presence, has its own content, and doesn't depend on the website for growth. Genuinely fine for simple needs — just know what you're (not) getting.

### Tier 2: Professional — LKR 80,000–150,000

**What it typically is:** A more customised site with better design and some added services.

**What's usually included:**
- Customised design (template-based but more tailored, or lightly custom)
- More pages
- Mobile responsive
- Contact forms and basic integrations (WhatsApp, maps)
- Basic SEO setup
- Some content support or guidance
- A round or two of revisions
- Basic testing

**What to check:**
- How custom is the design, really?
- Is SEO genuinely set up, or just mentioned?
- Who writes the content?
- What support comes after launch?

**Right for:** Small businesses that want a professional, somewhat distinctive site and basic SEO, without a large budget. A common sweet spot for Sri Lankan SMEs.

### Tier 3: Custom — LKR 200,000–400,000

**What it typically is:** A genuinely custom-designed, multi-page website built for your specific business.

**What's usually included:**
- Fully custom design (not a template)
- Multiple pages, including individual service pages
- Strong mobile-first design
- Proper SEO foundations (keyword-targeted pages, schema, Search Console setup)
- Content support or writing
- Integrations (forms, WhatsApp, CRM, analytics)
- Thorough testing across devices and browsers
- Multiple revision rounds
- Training on managing the site
- Post-launch support period

**Right for:** Businesses where the website is a serious asset — you want to rank on Google, stand out from competitors, offer multiple services, and grow. This tier is where the site becomes a genuine growth tool rather than a digital business card.

### Tier 4: Premium — LKR 500,000–800,000+

**What it typically is:** A premium, custom build with advanced design, functionality, or complexity.

**What's usually included:**
- Premium custom design, often with a modern high-performance foundation (see our [Next.js vs WordPress guide](/blog/nextjs-vs-wordpress-sri-lanka/))
- Extensive pages and content
- Advanced functionality (custom features, complex integrations, ecommerce)
- Comprehensive SEO strategy
- Professional content production, possibly photography
- Advanced testing and optimisation
- Extended support and often ongoing partnership

**Right for:** Established businesses, funded startups, and organisations where the website is central to the business and needs to compete at a high level — performance, design, functionality, and brand all operating at a premium standard.

## How to compare quotes honestly

When you get multiple quotes, don't compare prices — compare inclusions. Ask every provider to specify:

1. **Custom or template design?**
2. **How many pages?**
3. **Who writes the content?** (A huge hidden cost — copywriting can be LKR 4,000–25,000 per page.)
4. **Is SEO genuinely set up?** (Keyword research, schema, Search Console, or just "SEO-friendly"?)
5. **What integrations are included?** (Forms, WhatsApp, payment, CRM, analytics.)
6. **How much testing?**
7. **How many revisions?**
8. **What post-launch support?**
9. **Who owns the site and files afterward?**
10. **What's explicitly NOT included?**

A cheaper quote missing content, SEO, testing, and support isn't cheaper — it's a smaller product. When you add the excluded items back (paying separately later), it often costs more than the "expensive" quote that included them.

## The hidden costs to watch for

Packages often look cheaper by excluding things you'll need anyway:

- **Content writing** — the biggest hidden cost. "You supply content" sounds fine until you realise writing 12 pages of professional copy is a major job.
- **SEO** — "SEO-friendly" is not the same as SEO setup. Real SEO (keyword-targeted pages, schema, Search Console) is work that some packages skip entirely.
- **Photography** — stock or your own? Professional photography is a real cost.
- **Post-launch support and maintenance** — see our [website maintenance cost guide](/blog/website-maintenance-cost-sri-lanka/). Every site needs it; not every package includes it.
- **Revisions beyond the included rounds** — extra revisions can add up.
- **Training** — will you know how to update the site?

## Matching the package to your business

Choose your tier based on honest answers to:

- **How much does the website matter to your business?** Critical → higher tier. Nice-to-have → lower tier.
- **Do you need to rank on Google?** Yes → you need real SEO (Tier 3+).
- **Do you have your own content, or need help?** Need help → factor in content cost.
- **How much do you need to stand out?** A lot → custom design (Tier 3+). Not much → template is fine.
- **Will you grow?** Significantly → build for it now to avoid rebuilding.

## The honest recommendation

There's no universally "right" package — there's a right package for your business, your goals, and your budget.

- **Simple presence, own content, tight budget:** Tier 1–2
- **Professional site with basic SEO:** Tier 2
- **Serious growth tool — rank, convert, stand out:** Tier 3
- **Premium, complex, or competitive at a high level:** Tier 4

The two mistakes are equally common: overpaying for a premium package when a professional one would do, and underbuying a budget package that excludes the SEO and custom design your growth actually depends on — then rebuilding a year later.

Compare inclusions, not headline prices. Match the tier to your real needs. And insist on transparency — any provider unwilling to itemise exactly what's included (and excluded) is hiding something you'd rather know before you pay.
