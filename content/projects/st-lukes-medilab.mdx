## The challenge

St Luke's Medical Laboratory is a single independent diagnostic lab in Ja-Ela, competing for the same patients as Sri Lanka's national lab chains. It has the smaller marketing budget and the smaller brand. The website had to bring patients in directly from Google and WhatsApp, not just act as a brochure.

## The strategy: three advantages the chains don't use

**Price transparency.** We published 58 test prices in LKR across 12 categories, from LKR 100 to 7,000, in a searchable price finder with category filters and a preparation guide for each test, plus 4 bundled health packages.

**Hyper-local SEO.** 10 town landing pages cover 7 walk-in towns and 3 home-visit service areas, from Ja-Ela to Negombo. Each has its own intro, landmarks, neighbourhoods, local FAQs and a Google Maps embed. The Katunayake page targets pre-employment and visa medicals for airport and Free Trade Zone workers.

**Technical SEO.** 19 test-price landing pages, 22 schema.org types (including `MedicalBusiness`, `MedicalTest`, `MedicalDevice`, `FAQPage` and `OfferCatalog`), canonical URLs, one keyword-focused H1 per page, an automatic sitemap and auto-generated share images for every page.

## Content marketing

20 articles and about 21,400 words so far, 3 of them in Sinhala, each reviewed by a qualified medical lab technologist named in the byline. During the June 2026 dengue spike we published dengue articles in English and Sinhala while people were searching.

## Results so far

- Lighthouse SEO 100/100 on mobile and desktop
- Best Practices 100/100, Accessibility 97/100
- Desktop performance 100/100, 0.5 s load, zero layout shift
- 36% less JavaScript (1.4 MB → 0.9 MB)

> The work continues. We have managed the site since February 2026, and new articles, towns and services go live by editing a single file.
