## The Brief

Coventry Business Club is one of the UK Midlands' established business networks, bringing together managing directors, founders, and civic leaders. Their previous digital presence failed to convey the exclusivity and vitality of the physical club, resulting in friction when announcing signature events or onboarding new members.

## The Approach

We took inspiration from high-end modern members' clubs — replacing sterile corporate templates with an editorial layout that celebrates member achievements and community leadership.

### Editorial Architecture

- **Event Showcase**: A clear calendar view of upcoming roundtables with one-click RSVP.
- **Member Directory**: Categorized index facilitating peer-to-peer business collaboration.
- **Prestige Narrative**: Testimonials and historical milestones presented with editorial grace.

## The Result

The redesigned platform positions Coventry Business Club as a forward-thinking business collective, driving membership inquiries and elevating executive engagement.
