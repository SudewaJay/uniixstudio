## The Brief

Wasana Drivers needed a digital home that offered the convenience and credibility of major ride apps without the overhead and commission cuts. International travelers arriving in Sri Lanka seek trustworthy, English-fluent chauffeurs with clear vehicle standards and upfront communication.

## The Approach

We prioritized mobile ergonomics above all else. Most users book while in transit — on airport Wi-Fi, low-bandwidth 4G, or from hotels. We built an ultra-fast, streamlined booking interface that eliminates lengthy sign-up forms in favor of direct, verified communication.

### Trust-Centric Architecture

1. **Clear Vehicle Standards**: Upfront photos and luggage capacities for sedans, vans, and SUVs.
2. **Airport Transfer Precision**: Step-by-step pickup instructions that eliminate arrival anxiety.
3. **Direct Contact**: Instant connection via WhatsApp and phone to confirm itineraries in real time.

## The Result

The site provides an authoritative, direct-to-driver booking channel that converts search traffic into confirmed itineraries across Sri Lanka.
