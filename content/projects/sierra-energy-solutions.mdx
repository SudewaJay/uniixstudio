**Sierra Energy Solutions** is a Sri Lankan renewable-energy company helping homes and businesses move to solar. Great product, growing demand — but a social feed that didn't yet look the part. We gave the brand a social media design system built around the calendar.

Rather than one-off graphics, we created a fixed brand frame — the Sierra logo lockup, a blue-to-green palette drawn from the identity, and a `sierraenergy.lk` footer band — and wrapped it around bold, occasion-specific artwork. World Science Day leans on solar panels and wind turbines; Universal Children's Day on a warm, family-and-future scene; the New Year on a celebratory Colombo skyline in Sinhala.

The result is a run of posts that feel distinct moment to moment but unmistakably *Sierra* at a glance — a bilingual, feed-ready template the brand can reuse for any date on the calendar.
