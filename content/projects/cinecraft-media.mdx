Cinecraft Media is a creative video production studio in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Its identity, designed by Uniix Studio, centres on a bold, interlocking CM monogram paired with a letter-spaced CINECRAFT wordmark and a smaller MEDIA line in a gold gradient. The identity was delivered as a full logo and a standalone monogram, each in light and dark versions.
