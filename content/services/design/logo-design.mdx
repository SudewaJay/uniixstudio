## A logo that earns its place on every surface

A logo's job is not to look beautiful in isolation — it's to do credible work on a vehicle decal at 40 metres, a WhatsApp avatar at 96 pixels, a printed brochure on uncoated stock, and a 3-second TikTok reveal. A logo that wins the Behance presentation but loses the WhatsApp profile is a logo that hasn't been finished.

We design logos that hold up across the surfaces your business actually uses, not just the ones that look good in a hero shot. Wordmarks, symbols, full lockups — built with optical balance, real-world weight tuning, and the usage rules that keep the mark from drifting six months later.

## Why a strong logo matters more in 2026 than in 2016

Three structural shifts have raised the bar:

1. **More surfaces.** A 2016 logo had to work on a business card, a website header and maybe a Facebook profile. A 2026 logo lives on TikTok cover frames, vertical Reels, app icons, AR overlays, WhatsApp Business profiles, Google Business Profile, Apple Maps cards, AI-search result cards and printed material — each with its own constraints.
2. **AI rendered competition.** Competitors can now generate "passable" logos for under USD 50 in 10 minutes. A logo that doesn't have genuine strategic intent and craft behind it now reads as templated — because so much of what surrounds it actually is.
3. **Compressed attention.** A vertical-video viewer decides whether to keep watching in 1.5 seconds. The logo reveal has to land instantly or it doesn't land at all.

The brands compounding fastest in 2026 are not the ones with the most decorated logos. They're the ones with marks that are **strategically intentional, application-tested, and consistently used.**

## How a logo fits into the larger brand picture

A logo is one component of a brand identity, not the whole thing. We work on logos as standalone engagements when:

- The business already has a strong [brand identity](/services/design/brand-identity/) and only needs the mark refreshed
- The business is pre-strategy and needs a credible mark to launch with, with full identity to follow
- The business has a single logo problem (e.g., the mark doesn't scale down) that doesn't justify a full rebrand

For anything broader — palette, typography, voice, social system — see our [brand identity](/services/design/brand-identity/) service. For brands building the rest of the visual system on top of a strong logo, our [social media creatives](/services/design/social-media-creatives/), [print design](/services/design/print-design/) and [motion graphics](/services/design/motion-graphics/) services apply the logo system across every weekly surface.

## What separates our logo work

- **Real applications in exploration.** Each direction is shown on a vehicle, a WhatsApp avatar, a business card and an app icon before we choose. We judge the system, not the swatch.
- **Optical balance, not just geometric centring.** We hand-tune weight, spacing and stroke to feel right at the sizes the logo will actually be used.
- **Equity-aware redesigns.** When evolving an existing logo, we identify the silhouette, colour and visual cues that customers already recognise — and we keep them. A redesign that throws away earned brand recognition is usually a mistake.
- **Usage rules that prevent drift.** Every logo ships with a one-page rules sheet that's linkable, screenshot-able, and impossible to misplace. Your team, freelancers and partners all work from the same source.
