## SEO that compounds — for the SEO, AEO and GEO surfaces

Search in 2026 isn't one surface — it's three. Classic Google ranking still drives most commercial intent. Google's AI Overview and People Also Ask capture an increasing share of informational queries. And ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude now generate real referral traffic for businesses cited as authoritative sources. The SEO programmes that win in 2026 target all three.

We run SEO engagements engineered for compounding — pillar pages built for high-intent commercial queries, supporting clusters for topical authority, technical foundations that don't break, and content structured for AI citation. The unit economics flip dramatically positive by month 9-12; before that, the work is investment.

For the strategic framework on when SEO is the right call vs PPC, see our [PPC vs SEO 2026 guide](/blog/ppc-vs-seo-which-should-you-choose-2026/).

## What SEO actually covers in 2026

Five disciplines that compose modern SEO — we run all of them as one programme:

1. **Technical SEO** — Core Web Vitals, schema, internal linking, indexability, sitemap, redirects
2. **On-page SEO** — content structure, headings, internal linking, image alt, page speed
3. **Content SEO** — pillar pages, cluster strategy, topical depth, intent matching
4. **Local SEO** — Google Business Profile, citations, reviews, location pages (for Sri Lankan businesses serving specific areas)
5. **AEO + GEO** — FAQ schema, direct-answer summaries, AI-assistant citation optimisation

Most Sri Lankan SEO agencies still treat these as separate. We treat them as one engagement because they compound together — and break apart when fragmented.

## Programmatic publishing — the 2026 unit economics

The biggest shift in SEO unit economics in the past 18 months is **programmatic content publishing**. AI scaffolds the research and structure; a human expert adds judgment, sources, and opinion; the WordPress or Next.js CMS publishes automatically; RankMath or Yoast handles on-page SEO.

We built a programmatic pipeline for RentMyCar.lk in 2025 using Python + Gemini + WP REST + RankMath on cron. The economics: dramatically more articles shipped per month at substantially lower cost per article, with quality maintained because the human stays in the loop on judgment and sources. For SEO Programme clients we offer this pipeline as a managed service.
