# Digital PR & Backlink Targets — Uniix Studio

> Built to close the biggest remaining gap in the site's SEO/GEO profile: external authority signals from third-party domains. Backlinks compound for years and reduce dependence on internal-link substitutes.

## How to use this doc

1. **Tier 1** outlets are the highest-priority — earning even 2-3 placements here over the next 6 months would materially move domain authority. Pitch with the strongest single angle per outlet.
2. **Tier 2** outlets are easier-to-land contributions that build cumulative authority. Aim for 1-2 placements per quarter.
3. **Tier 3** outlets are local communities and niche directories that take less effort but produce relevant link equity.
4. Each pitch angle is paired with the destination page on uniixstudio.com it should link to — that's the page that compounds.

## Tier 1 — National Sri Lankan business media

The bar is high; one placement compounds for years.

### 1. Daily FT — `ft.lk`
- **Section to pitch:** Business, Tech, Marketing, SME
- **Editor approach:** Contributor articles + expert commentary in news stories
- **Pitch angles:**
  - *"The 2026 State of Branding in Sri Lanka"* — pitch the existing [state-of-branding report](https://www.uniixstudio.com/blog/state-of-branding-sri-lanka-2026/) as an op-ed
  - *"Sri Lankan SMEs and the AI search shift"* — original analysis from the [digital trends report](https://www.uniixstudio.com/blog/uniix-studio-2026-digital-trends-report/)
  - *"Why most Sri Lankan brands lose money on Meta Ads"* — original data from the [PPC vs SEO analysis](https://www.uniixstudio.com/blog/ppc-vs-seo-which-should-you-choose-2026/)
- **Link target:** Blog post URL referenced in the article

### 2. LMD (Lanka Monthly Digest) — `lmd.lk`
- **Section to pitch:** Business Today, Marketing, Tech Lanka
- **Editor approach:** Monthly print + online; long-lead-time contributions
- **Pitch angles:**
  - *"The 2026 Sri Lankan Branding Benchmark"* — original survey data + commentary
  - *"From WhatsApp to Marketplace: How Sri Lankan Ecommerce Grew Up"* — anchored to the [ecommerce guide](https://www.uniixstudio.com/blog/ecommerce-website-development-sri-lanka-guide/)
- **Link target:** Author bio + 1 in-article link to a relevant blog post

### 3. Echelon — `echelon.lk`
- **Section to pitch:** Tech, Business Strategy, Innovation
- **Editor approach:** Premium business journal; bylined long-form
- **Pitch angles:**
  - *"What AI Search Means for Sri Lankan B2B Brands"* — strategic analysis, not vendor pitch
  - *"The Discipline Gap: Why Sri Lankan Brand Consistency Lags Regional Peers"*
- **Link target:** Author bio + 1-2 strategic links

### 4. Daily Mirror Business — `dailymirror.lk/business`
- **Section to pitch:** Business, Tech
- **Editor approach:** News-led; pitch expert commentary on emerging topics
- **Pitch angles:**
  - Quote-able expert commentary on Sri Lankan digital advertising trends
  - Original data on Sri Lankan ecommerce performance benchmarks
- **Link target:** Quote attribution links

### 5. The Morning / Sunday Morning — `themorning.lk`
- **Section to pitch:** Business, Tech, Tribune
- **Editor approach:** Op-ed and expert-byline friendly
- **Pitch angles:**
  - *"What Every Sri Lankan SME Should Know About Local SEO in 2026"*
  - *"The Cost of Bad Branding for Small Businesses"*
- **Link target:** Mentions of /services/growth/seo/ and /services/design/brand-identity/

## Tracking and measurement

For each placement that lands:

1. **Add to a master sheet** with: outlet, URL, date, anchor text, target page, follow vs nofollow status
2. **Set up Google Alerts** for `"Uniix Studio"` and `"uniixstudio.com"` to catch unsolicited mentions
3. **Monthly review** of new referring domains in Ahrefs / Semrush / Google Search Console
4. **Quarterly review** against domain authority growth and ranking impact

Target trajectory: 2-3 Tier 1 placements + 6-8 Tier 2 placements in the first 12 months. That alone would move uniixstudio.com from a near-zero external backlink profile to a credible domain in the Sri Lankan agency competitive set.

The internal-link cluster we've built (services ↔ blog ↔ portfolio) gives us a strong substitute while external backlinks build — but the external layer is the unlock for ranking on the high-volume `"digital agency Sri Lanka"` and `"creative agency Colombo"` queries where domain authority remains the dominant factor.
